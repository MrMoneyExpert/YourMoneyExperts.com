The 3 Wealth Gaps

Hidden Tax Bombs Most Families Never See Coming

These three gaps silently destroy retirement wealth. The Wealth Gap Audit exposes all three — at no cost.

Gap 01
The Social Security Tax Trap
If your Bucket 2 withdrawals push your income over the threshold, up to 85% of your Social Security check gets taxed. Most people have no idea this exists until it's too late.

Gap 02
Medicare IRMAA Surcharges
High 401k withdrawals trigger Medicare surcharges that can cost thousands per year in additional premiums. The 2026 bracket starts at $109k single / $218k joint income.
